Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions.

What areas do you cover?

PRIMESHIELD SOLUTIONS LIMITED is based in Mombasa, Kenya, and we proudly serve clients across the coastal region and nationwide. We deploy both static guarding and rapid response teams wherever our clients need us.

Are your security guards licensed and trained?

Yes, all our personnel are carefully selected, fully licensed, and undergo rigorous, ongoing training. We invest in leadership development and operational systems to ensure every officer meets our high standards of professionalism.

Do you offer 24/7 support and alarm response?

Absolutely. Our alarm response services and emergency support are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. We ensure rapid deployment to any alarm activation or emergency situation.

Can I upgrade my existing CCTV system?

Yes, we provide comprehensive CCTV system design, installation, and maintenance. We can assess your current setup and integrate modern security technology to improve efficiency and situational awareness.

How do I request a security assessment for my premises?

You can request a consultation by filling out the contact form on our website or calling us directly. Our team will schedule a convenient time to visit your site, identify vulnerabilities, and provide practical security recommendations.

What industries do you serve?

We serve a wide range of sectors including residential communities, commercial buildings, construction projects, hospitality establishments, religious institutions, industrial facilities, and retail businesses.

What happens if an alarm is triggered at my property?

If an alarm is triggered, our 24/7 monitoring center is instantly notified. We immediately dispatch our rapid response team to your location and follow the agreed-upon incident protocols to secure the premises.

Can I get a customized security plan?

Absolutely. We do not believe in a one-size-fits-all approach. Every client receives a tailored security solution designed around their unique security requirements and operational environment.
