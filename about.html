About PRIMESHIELD

Built on Trust.
Driven by Excellence.

01 — Who We Are

Creating safe environments where businesses and communities can thrive.

PRIMESHIELD SOLUTIONS LIMITED is a Kenyan security and risk solutions company dedicated to protecting people, property, and businesses through professional security services, operational excellence, and innovative technology.

We believe security goes beyond deploying guards—it is about creating safe environments where businesses and communities can operate with confidence. By combining highly trained personnel, disciplined management, modern technology, and customer-focused service, we deliver tailored security solutions that meet the evolving needs of our clients.

Vision

To become Kenya's most trusted security company.

Setting the benchmark for excellence, innovation, and client satisfaction—recognized as the standard by which other security providers are measured.

Mission

To deliver dependable risk management solutions.

Through highly trained personnel, advanced technology, and exceptional customer service while building lasting relationships founded on trust, integrity, and professionalism.

02 — Core Values

The principles behind every operation.

Integrity

We conduct our business honestly, ethically, and transparently. Every action is accountable, every promise is kept.

Professionalism

We uphold the highest standards in appearance, conduct, and service delivery. Excellence is the default, not the exception.

Trust

We build long-term relationships through reliability, accountability, and consistency. Trust is earned daily, never assumed.

Excellence

We continuously improve our people, systems, and services. Standing still is moving backward—we choose forward.

Vigilance

We remain alert, proactive, and prepared to respond to emerging risks. The threat never sleeps—neither does our readiness.

Teamwork

We believe collaboration produces better outcomes for our clients. No officer stands alone—we operate as one disciplined unit.

03 — Our Team

Led by experience.
Driven by excellence.

PRIMESHIELD SOLUTIONS LIMITED is led by experienced professionals committed to operational excellence, ethical leadership, and continuous improvement.

Our management team works closely with supervisors and frontline security personnel to ensure every assignment is executed with professionalism, accountability, and attention to detail.
